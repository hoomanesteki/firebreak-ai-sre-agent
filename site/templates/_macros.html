These pages show a reason wherever a number is not yet quotable. Firebreak is built and tested end to end, and its evaluation harness is reporting that it has nothing quotable to say yet. Every figure in reports/eval/ carries the reason it cannot be quoted, and these pages repeat the reason rather than the figure, which is the whole job of the gate. Two inputs change that and neither is an engineering task: model credentials, and recordings for the held-out splits.
