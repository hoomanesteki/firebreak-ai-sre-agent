Why there is no form here yet

Feedback is recorded through firebreak.memory.feedback, and the store and its rules exist and are tested. A form is a view over them, and building one before any report has been reviewed would be building a view over nothing. What the form has to enforce is already enforced: a report marked wrong with no correction is refused, because it cannot become an evaluation task.
