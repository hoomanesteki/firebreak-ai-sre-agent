# Phase 7: Critic, hypothesis board, exit gate, abstention

## Summary

The exit gate is built, all six checks, and it cannot be bypassed: every route
out of `investigate` returns through the one function that runs it. Baselines B1
and B2 and ablation A1 exist, and configurations can now be compared on the same
tasks with a paired interval, which is the form SPEC.md Section 9.4 requires.

Two things did not go to plan and both are recorded rather than worked around.

**The abstain threshold cannot be tuned yet, and the reason is now measured
rather than suspected.** On the eight clean validation recordings the shipped
threshold abstains on five of seven real faults. A re-tune would more than double
sensitivity at no measured cost in specificity, and it is still the wrong move:
there is one no-fault recording, three of the seven positives sit exactly at the
score cap, and the two faults that a re-tune would still miss are both latency
faults scoring below the healthy recording. That last point is the finding. No
threshold on top anomaly z can catch a latency fault without abstaining on a busy
healthy system, so this rule needs a second signal, not a better number.

**Six of the fourteen recordings made so far were corrupt, and nothing had
noticed.** The machine had taken macOS maintenance sleep during overnight
recording. The files were intact, the checksums matched, the manifests were
complete, and the recordings had holes in them of 315 to 5010 seconds against a
15 second sampling cadence. They are deleted, the recorder now refuses to produce
one, and the library is eight bundles rather than fourteen.

## What was built

**The exit gate** (`src/firebreak/agent/gates.py`). Six checks per SPEC.md
Section 6.9, each returning a named result with what it examined and what it
rejected. One repair pass, then removal, then a re-check, because removing claims
can break a check that passed on the larger report. Abstention is applied rather
than repaired: it clears the root cause and keeps the claims. ADR-0010 records
the decisions.

**Re-execution** (`src/firebreak/agent/reexecute.py`). Check 2 re-runs each cited
record through the tool that produced it, in a fresh tool context over the same
backend. Evidence records now carry the tool name and its validated arguments,
stamped by `ToolRegistry.call` rather than by each of the fourteen tools.

**The gate in the control flow.** `_finish` in `agent/graph.py` is the single
funnel every path returns through, and it runs the gate. The Phase 6 version
called it once at the end of the main loop, so a malformed incident and an
abstaining triage returned reports nothing had checked.

**`Report.notes`.** Code-written procedural text, which the gate does not check
and the reporter model cannot write, because `ReporterOutput` has no such field.
The two code-written reports use it.

**Baseline B1** (`src/firebreak/agent/react.py`). One agent, every tool except
the graph ones, the strong tier on every call, the same caps and the same budget
limits. **B2** is the same function with `gate=True`. **Ablation A1** is
`investigate(use_critic=False)`.

**Paired comparison** (`src/firebreak/evals/compare.py`, `firebreak eval
compare`). Reports now carry per-task scores, which SPEC.md Section 9.6 asked for
and they did not have. Pairing is by bundle id, never by position.

**Recorder protections** (`lab/bundle.py`, `lab/recorder.py`,
`scripts/record_library.py`). `check_continuity` measures the largest gap between
metric samples against a cadence measured from the bundle itself and refuses a
recording with a hole. `check_duration` compares the window against the
scenario's planned total. `record_library` holds a `caffeinate` assertion and
warns when on battery, and records the no-fault scenarios in each split first,
because they are the scarce resource the abstention threshold needs.

What those numbers do and do not say:

- **The one interval that excludes zero is FB's top-3 advantage over B1**, and it
  is partly by construction: B1 reports no ranked candidates because it has no
  ranking, and inventing one from the anomaly scores it happened to see would
  hand it a metric it did not earn. It reads as "B1 offers no alternatives" more
  than "FB ranks well".
- **B1 beats FB on abstention by 0.5**, and the interval's upper bound sits
  exactly on zero, so the data does not quite establish it. The point estimate is
  the abstention problem stated as a number: a baseline that never abstains does
  better than FB, because FB abstains on six of eight real incidents.
- **FB equals B0 on every grader.** On these eight bundles the agent adds nothing
  to deterministic triage. Expected in stub mode and kept in the report, per
  SPEC.md Section 9.5's rule that results favouring simpler configurations are
  not hidden.
- **B2 equals B1 exactly, and FB equals A1 exactly.** The stub never fabricates,
  so the gate has nothing to remove; the stub critic applies one keyword rule, so
  it changes no outcome. Neither row is a finding about the gate or the critic.
  The machinery is what was built; the numbers arrive with credentials.
- `evidence_validity` covers two tasks for FB and eight for B1 because an
  abstaining FB report cites nothing.

## Deviations from the spec (with ADR links)

**Blast radius is checked for provenance, not recomputed** (ADR-0010). Section
6.9 check 4 says the blast radius matches the graph query. The gate holds no
graph client by design, so it verifies that a blast radius claim cites a topology
record and lets check 3 verify its numbers against that record. A blast radius
asserted from prose fails, which is the failure the clause exists for.

**B2's notebook is translated from its report** (ADR-0010). Checks 4 and 6 read a
notebook and a single agent has none. An empty one would make check 6 abstain on
every incident, so B2 would score zero and measure nothing. B2's abstention rate
is therefore a claim about how much evidence B1 cited.

**B1 excludes the dependency tools, not only the ranking.** Section 9.5 gives B1
"no graph ranking" and A2 "no ranking, no dependency tools". Leaving the
dependency tools in B1 would make it a weaker A2, and the two rows would stop
answering different questions.

**ADR-0008's Phase 6 text said it would be finalised in Phase 9.** SPEC.md
Section 17 says Phase 7. Corrected in the record.

## Known issues

**The abstain threshold is known wrong and cannot yet be replaced.** Measured
above. It needs more no-fault recordings, which the recording order now
prioritises, and a latency-specific signal, which no threshold on top anomaly z
can substitute for.

**The ranking blames the frontend for payment failures.** All three
payment-failure recordings name `frontend` as the top candidate with `payment`
in the top three. That is the symptom-over-cause failure the graph walk exists to
prevent, and on real telemetry it is not preventing it. Worth a measurement pass
of its own once more of the library is recorded.

**B1 names observability infrastructure.** One bundle gets `jaeger`, because the
`NOT_UNDER_INVESTIGATION` filter lives in the ranking stage B1 does not have.
This is a real cost of the simpler design and is kept, but the stub's plan is
mine rather than a model's, and a prompted model might well exclude infra
without being told to. Treat the row as a property of this B1 implementation.

**An almost empty bundle is still accepted.** `check_continuity` passes a bundle
with too few samples to establish a cadence, because "cannot tell" is not "has a
hole". Nothing else rejects an empty bundle either. A continuity check is the
wrong place to hide an emptiness check, so this is noted rather than smuggled in.

**The library is eight bundles.** Six were deleted as corrupt and five of those
scenarios are queued to record again.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Model credentials.** `LLM_BASE_URL` and `LLM_API_KEY`, or a local Ollama.
   Three Phase 6 acceptance criteria and most of this phase's comparison depend
   on them. Everything downstream is built and will produce real numbers the day
   they exist.
2. **AC power for recording.** The machine is on battery, and macOS takes
   maintenance sleep on battery whatever `caffeinate` asks for. That is what
   corrupted six bundles. Recording is stopped rather than restarted, because on
   battery every attempt would now be correctly discarded and retried, burning
   the battery to produce nothing. About 30 hours of recording remain.
3. **Where ground truth lives.** `labels/` is still untracked. `package_library`
   archives bundles only. Repository or release archive is a decision about who
   can reproduce a number.
4. **`gh auth login`**, so the phase PR can be opened.
